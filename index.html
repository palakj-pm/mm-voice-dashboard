Two AI voice agents (Riya at delivery, Neha at month-1) call Man Matters customers to drive the reorder. Here is what the data says.
📈
Both agents lift reordersMeasured cleanly, Neha reorders ~1.8× vs control (19.2% vs 10.6%, early-maturity) and Riya 1.46× — both with a clean completed > dropped > rest gradient.
🔬
Riya works downstream — reconsider pausing herThe paused onboarding agent drives 2.6× habit-tracker, 1.76× app opens, 1.16× doctor consults and 1.46× reorders vs control — monotonic across all four.
⏳
Measure at maturity, on a clean windowLift looks huge at 14 days (2.3×) and “vanishes” on a dirty long window — first orders inflate the control to 41%. Truth = a real but smaller lift; use reorders 10–45 days after the call.
🚀
Biggest lever: go cheap, then prove itMost customers reorder anyway, so WhatsApp-first (Journey B, now live) beats cold-calling everyone. Confirm the true causal lift with a randomised don't-call holdout.

Why they didn't reorder — from the calls 🧮 transcript-verified

Every non-reordering completed customer's call was read and categorised (not the auto-tag).
The real breakdown: ~41% simply aren't due yet (still have stock / already bought a long pack), and ~37% were ready but no order was placed on the call. Genuine dissatisfaction is only ~17%. So the fix is closing the sale on the call, not fighting churn.
